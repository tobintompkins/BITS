# Member Portal Annual Giving Summary

The private Member & Donor Portal supports a selected-year giving view on
`/portal/statements`.

- Members and guest donors see only the donor record connected to their signed-in account.
- The year selector supports the current year and five prior years.
- Annual totals show gift count, total giving, tax-deductible giving, and giving by fund.
- Stripe sandbox gifts remain visible as test receipts but are excluded from annual totals.
- Receipt queries are restricted to the selected calendar year.

Published individual and authorized household statement PDFs are listed on the
same page. See `docs/member-portal-statement-pdf-access.md` and
`docs/member-portal-household-statement-authorization.md`.
